Journey Through the Ear一声入耳

Make a sound and ride with it: pinna → eardrum → ossicles → cochlea → hair cells → auditory nerve. At every stop, predict, act on the model, then explain.发出一个声音，跟着它走：耳廓 → 鼓膜 → 听小骨 → 耳蜗 → 毛细胞 → 听神经。每一站先预测、在模型上动手，再解释。

Sound is off by default. Turn it on with the speaker in the top bar.声音默认关闭，可用顶部扬声器按钮开启。
